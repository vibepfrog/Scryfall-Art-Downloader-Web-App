Scryfall Scans Mass Downloading Web App.

Everything you see on screen is what it will download.
The site loads 175 images at a time, you can hit the "deselect all" button to skip to loading the next lot of 175, (quicker than scrolling all the way to the bottom) just hit "Select All" after to make them all visible again.
Finally just hit "Download Selected" to download a zip of all of the scans.
